How to play

Three simple rules. One very dramatic duck.

↔️

Switch lanes

Move left and right to avoid hazards. Watch the whole track instead of staring at one obstacle.

⬆️

Jump at the right time

Jump over low hazards. A jump has a short cooldown, so time it carefully.

⭐

Collect stars

Stars add to your arcade score. They are just for fun and are not added to your DUK LEAGUE RP.
