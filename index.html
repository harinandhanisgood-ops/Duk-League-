OFFICIAL DOCUMENT

THE LEAGUE RULES.

Every member is expected to follow these rules. Keep DUK LEAGUE fair, organized and enjoyable.

01

RESPECT EVERY MEMBER

Treat other members fairly. Harassment, bullying, discrimination and unnecessary arguments are not acceptable.

02

RESPECT ADMINISTRATORS

Administrators help manage the League. Follow reasonable instructions and report problems properly.

03

NO CHEATING

Do not exploit League systems, manipulate scores, impersonate another member or use unfair methods.

04

PROTECT PRIVATE INFORMATION

Do not share another member's personal details or private conversations without permission.

05

NO IMPERSONATION

Do not pretend to be an administrator, owner or another League member.

06

USE THE ARCADE FAIRLY

Do not use modified game files or claim fake records as legitimate achievements.

07

RESPECT TITLES AND RANKS

Titles and ranks must be assigned by authorized administrators. Members must not grant themselves positions.

08

DO NOT ABUSE ACCESS CODES

Access codes are intended for League members. Do not share them publicly.

09

KEEP THE COMMUNITY ENJOYABLE

Help other members, cooperate and contribute positively to League activities.

10

CONSEQUENCES

Serious or repeated violations may result in warnings, temporary restrictions or removal from DUK LEAGUE. Decisions should be handled fairly.

THE LEAGUE IS WHAT WE MAKE IT.

Respect the members. Respect the rules. Keep the community going.
